Privacy Policy — Gamer Trivia

App: Gamer Trivia · Package: com.pouter.gamertrivia · Developer: Pouter · Effective: 30 October 2026

What this app does

Gamer Trivia is a gaming-themed multiple-choice trivia game with Quick Game and Challenge modes, local high scores, optional Google sign-in with a Firebase-backed leaderboard, and rewarded ads that grant extra chances.

Data stored on your device

High scores, preferences (theme, audio), and the signed-in profile cache are stored only on your device using app storage. Clearing the app's storage removes this data.

Data collected and shared

The app itself sends nothing to us. Third-party SDKs collect and share the following:

  • Google sign-in (optional): if you sign in, your name, email address, and profile picture are used to identify your leaderboard entry and are stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Firebase Firestore: your user ID, display name, profile picture, top score, total correct answers, and best streak are stored so the leaderboard works across devices. Data is encrypted in transit and retained until you request deletion.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): device or other IDs (e.g. Advertising ID), app interactions (ad views and taps), and crash logs/diagnostics, for advertising, fraud prevention, and analytics. All ad data is encrypted in transit and retained by Google under Google's policies.

Permissions

Internet access is used for ads, sign-in, and leaderboard sync. Audio and vibration are used for in-game sound effects and haptics. The app uses no camera, location, contacts, or sensor permissions.

Consent

Where required (including the EEA/UK), the app asks for ad consent through Google's UMP consent form before loading ads.

Request deletion of your data

To delete data associated with Gamer Trivia:

  1. On your Android device, go to Settings → Apps → Gamer Trivia → Storage → Clear data. This erases local scores, preferences, and the signed-in profile cache on the device.
  2. To reset the advertising identifier, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset or Delete Advertising ID.
  3. To delete your cloud leaderboard entry (name, email, profile picture, scores), email pouter.pro@gmail.com with the subject “Gamer Trivia — data deletion” and include the display name or email you signed in with. Your Firestore document is deleted within 30 days of a verified request.
  4. For help with ad data held by Google, include “Ad data” in the same email; ad data held by Google is deleted per Google's retention policies after your request is forwarded.

Children

Gamer Trivia is a general-audience trivia game and is not directed at children under 13. Users under 13 should not sign in or use leaderboard features.

Contact

Questions about this policy: pouter.pro@gmail.com.
