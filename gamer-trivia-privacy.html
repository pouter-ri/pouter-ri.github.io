Privacy Policy — Gamer Trivia

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Gamer Trivia ("the app") is a gaming trivia quiz app. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information (only if you sign in with Google): your name, email address, profile photo, and Google user ID. This is stored on your device and used to identify you on the leaderboard.
  • Leaderboard data (only if you sign in): your display name, high score, and game statistics (correct answers, best streak). This is stored in Google Firebase Firestore so the global leaderboard can be displayed to all players.
  • Game data on your device: high scores and settings stored locally on your phone. This never leaves your device.
  • Advertising ID: our ads provider (Google AdMob) may collect your device advertising ID to show and measure ads, including rewarded ads for extra lives.

Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone recordings, camera images, or files. The app requests no access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or storage.

How we use information

  • To operate the leaderboard and remember your scores.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Services / Google Sign-In — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Firestore) — Firebase Privacy
  • Google AdMob — AdMob & the Google privacy policy

Data retention and deletion

Local game data is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Signing out removes your saved profile from the device. To delete your leaderboard entry and associated account data from our servers, email us (see below) and we will delete it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
