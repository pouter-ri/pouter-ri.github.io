Privacy Policy — Unit Convert

App: Unit Convert · Package: com.pouter.unitconvert · Developer: Pouter · Effective: 26 October 2026

What this app does

Unit Convert is an offline-first unit converter (length, weight, temperature, volume, speed, and an offline currency estimate table). Conversions, favorites, and history are stored only on your device. The app offers no accounts and runs no developer servers.

Data collected and shared

The app itself sends nothing you type to us. The Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) included in the app collects and shares the following, for advertising, fraud prevention, and analytics:

  • Device or other IDs (e.g. Advertising ID)
  • App interactions (ad views and taps)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

All of this data is encrypted in transit. Ad data is retained by Google under Google's policies.

Consent

Where required (including the EEA/UK), the app asks for ad consent through Google's UMP consent form before loading ads.

Request deletion of your data

To delete data associated with Unit Convert:

  1. On your Android device, go to Settings → Apps → Unit Convert → Storage → Clear data. This erases local history and favorites on the device.
  2. To reset the advertising identifier, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset or Delete Advertising ID.
  3. For help with ad data held by Google, email pouter.pro@gmail.com with the subject “Unit Convert — data deletion”. Ad data held by Google is deleted per Google's retention policies after your request is forwarded.

Local data is removed immediately by step 1. Ad identifiers held by Google are handled under Google's advertising privacy policies.

Children

Unit Convert is not directed at children and targets users aged 18 and over.

Contact
